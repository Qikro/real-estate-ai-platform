## 4. Measurement & Governance Framework (Lens — Growth Analyst)

### North Star Metric
* **Activated Onboarded Firms ($A_{firm}$)**: A brokerage or developer asset management team completing **$\ge 2$ unique commercial deal runs** on `http://localhost:8000` within **7 days** of sandbox provisioning.
* **Baseline**: 0 active external accounts (greenfield launch).
* **Target**: 20 activated firms (12 Tier 1 + 8 Tier 2).

### Leading Diagnostics & System Guardrails
* **Time-to-First-Model (TTFM)**: Median time from sandbox credentials issued to completed 1st DCF run (Target: $< 24$ hours).
* **Funnel Velocity**: Teaser/Memo view-to-verification booking rate (Target: $\ge 20\%$ for Tier 1 direct).
* **Model Integrity Guardrail**: **$0.0\%$ calculation discrepancy tolerance**. Any mathematical or TS-RERA verification mismatch triggers an immediate outbound pause.

### Stop / Iterate / Scale Decision Thresholds
* **Day 15 Checkpoint**:
  * *Scale (Green)*: $\ge 6$ firms activated, TTFM $< 24$ hrs $\rightarrow$ authorize early activation of Tier 2 developers.
  * *Iterate (Amber)*: $3\text{--}5$ firms activated with drop-offs at rent roll ingestion $\rightarrow$ deploy 1-click template normalizer without changing messaging.
  * *Stop (Red)*: $< 2$ firms activated or any calculation discrepancy $\rightarrow$ freeze outbound immediately for audit.
* **Day 30 Review**:
  * *Scale*: $\ge 18$ activated firms $\rightarrow$ expand geographic scope to Central Hyderabad (Banjara Hills, Begumpet).
  * *Iterate*: $12\text{--}17$ activated firms with Tier 1 skew $\rightarrow$ dedicate 100% bandwidth to institutional brokerages.
  * *Kill*: $< 10$ activated firms $\rightarrow$ conduct root-cause review of core proposition.
